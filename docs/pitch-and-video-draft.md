## 3. A personal starting point
Enter your name, their name, and your signing hand. See which letters are eligible for live practice and which are reference-only. Everyone starts at lesson 1.1.

Speaker: “Names stay in this browser for onboarding and practice-word selection. The word service receives letter sets, not names. Optional notification subscriptions separately require consent.”

## 4. Measurement and coaching
MediaPipe supplies hand landmarks. The recognizer compares handshapes with recorded references. Gemini rewords measured feedback and suggests words from eligible letters.

Speaker: “Gemini does not grade signs. We describe a detection as matching the reference. We keep a local fallback when coaching is unavailable.”

## 5. Honest boundaries
Units 1 and 2 are the current scope. Moving letters and unvalidated letters are reference-only. This is not a full ASL course or a substitute for instruction from Deaf teachers.

Speaker: “No letters are currently enabled in PRACTICEABLE. Live grading needs the documented human letter gate before we show it as available. Greetings and conversations are coming later.”
